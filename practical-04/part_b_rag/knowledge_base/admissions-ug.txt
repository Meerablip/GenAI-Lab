Undergraduate admissions

Undergraduate applications for ZIT open on 3 January and close on 28 February each year. Applicants must have scored at least 72% in their secondary school examinations and must sit the ZIT Aptitude Test (ZAT), which is held on the second weekend of March at 40 test centres. The application fee is 45 USD, and it is waived for applicants from designated rural districts. Offer letters are released on 20 April, and admitted students must confirm their seat by paying the enrolment deposit before 5 May.
