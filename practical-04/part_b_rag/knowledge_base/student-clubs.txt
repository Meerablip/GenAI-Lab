Student clubs and festivals

ZIT has 38 student clubs. The robotics club, Gearheads, won the national championship in 2024. The music society, Resonance, organises the annual cultural festival Zephyria in the third week of February. The Coding Club, Segfault, runs a weekly hackathon every Thursday at 18:00 in Lab 4. Each club receives an annual budget of 1,200 USD. A new club needs at least 25 founding members and a faculty advisor to be recognised by the Student Council.
