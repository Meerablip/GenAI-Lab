Campus transport and parking

The free campus shuttle, the Orrin Loop, runs every 12 minutes between 06:45 and 21:00, connecting the main gate, the hostels, the library and Marrowfield Central Station. The ZipCycle bike-share has 300 bicycles, and the first 30 minutes of each ride are free. Student car parking permits cost 60 USD per year and are available only to final-year and postgraduate students. The speed limit inside the campus is 20 km/h.
