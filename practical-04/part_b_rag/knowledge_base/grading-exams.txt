Grading and examination rules

ZIT uses a 10-point grading scale: O (10 points) for 90 marks and above, A+ (9) for 80 to 89, A (8) for 70 to 79, B (7) for 60 to 69, C (6) for 50 to 59, and P (5) for 40 to 49. The pass mark is 40. Every course is assessed 40% through internal assessment and 60% through the end-semester examination. A student may request re-evaluation within 5 working days of the results at a cost of 12 USD per paper. Supplementary examinations are held in the first week of July. Students need 75% attendance to sit the end-semester examination; with a medical certificate, attendance down to 65% can be condoned.
