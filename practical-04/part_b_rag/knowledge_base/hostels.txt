Hostels and residence halls

ZIT has six residence halls on campus. Kestrel Hall is a men's hall with 480 beds and Linden Hall is a women's hall with 520 beds. The curfew is 22:30 on weekdays and 23:30 on Fridays and Saturdays. Visitors may stay in the common rooms only between 16:00 and 19:00. Rooms are allotted through a lottery held on the first Monday of July, and single rooms are reserved for final-year students. The hostel fee is 1,150 USD per year, billed separately from tuition and mess charges. Each hall has a resident warden and a laundry room open from 07:00 to 21:00.
