Campus safety and security

Campus security can be reached on extension 111. The main gate closes to visitors at 23:00, and student ID cards must be displayed at all times on campus. All entrances have CCTV coverage. Emergency assembly points are marked with green signs, and Assembly Point A is next to the Clock Tower. Fire drills are held twice a year, in September and February. Complaints of harassment are handled by the Internal Complaints Committee, chaired by Dr. Anjali Verhoeven.
