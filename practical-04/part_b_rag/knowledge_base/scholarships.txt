Scholarships and financial aid

ZIT offers three major scholarships. The Pennington Merit Scholarship waives 100% of tuition for the 15 entrants with the highest ZAT ranks. The Orrin Valley Grant gives a 40% tuition waiver to students from the Orrin Valley districts. The Women in STEM Award provides 2,500 USD per year to selected women students in engineering programmes. All scholarships are renewed annually only if the student maintains a CGPA of at least 8.0. Applications are handled by the Student Finance Office in Room B-114, and the deadline is 31 March.
