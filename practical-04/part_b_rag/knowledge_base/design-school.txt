Zephyr School of Design

The Zephyr School of Design offers a B.Des in Industrial Design and a B.Des in Interaction Design, with a combined intake of 120 seats. Admission to design programmes is based on a portfolio review instead of the ZAT. The fabrication studio, The Kiln, is open to design students from 10:00 to 20:00. The school holds its annual showcase, Prism, every November.
