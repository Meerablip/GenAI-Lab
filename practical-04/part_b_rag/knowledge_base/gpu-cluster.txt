Cumulus GPU cluster

The ZIT high-performance computing cluster is named Cumulus. It has 16 nodes, each with 4 NVIDIA A100 GPUs, for a total of 64 GPUs. Jobs are submitted through the Slurm scheduler. By default each student may use at most 24 GPU-hours per week. Project allocations of up to 400 GPU-hours require a sign-off from a faculty member at the Centre for Applied AI. The cluster has a maintenance window on every second Sunday of the month from 02:00 to 06:00, during which no jobs run.
