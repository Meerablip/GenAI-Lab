History and profile of ZIT

The Zephyr Institute of Technology (ZIT) was founded in 1987 by the industrialist Halvard Pennington as the Pennington Technical College in the river town of Marrowfield. In 2003 the college merged with the Zephyr School of Design and was renamed the Zephyr Institute of Technology. The main campus covers 212 acres on the banks of the Orrin River. The first vice-chancellor, Dr. Ilse Brandvold, led the institute until 1999. The institute's motto is "Ventus ad Futura", Latin for "wind towards the future". Current enrolment is about 9,400 students across undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes.
