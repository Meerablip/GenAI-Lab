Research centres

Research at ZIT is organised into three main centres. The Centre for Applied AI (CAAI), founded in 2016, is led by Dr. Naveen Okafor. The Centre for Coastal Engineering is led by Prof. Marisol Quintero, and the Materials Foundry is a shared fabrication facility. The annual research budget is 31 million USD, and 212 PhD scholars are currently enrolled. The flagship initiative, Project Lantern, develops low-cost flood-sensing buoys for the Orrin River and has deployed 58 buoys so far.
