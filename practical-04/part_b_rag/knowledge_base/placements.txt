Placements and internships

In the 2025 placement season, 91% of eligible ZIT students were placed. The median salary package was 54,000 USD and the highest was 148,000 USD, offered by Brightwater Systems. A total of 312 companies visited the campus. The placement season begins on 1 August, and students with an active backlog are not allowed to sit for placements. A mandatory internship of at least 8 weeks must be completed in the sixth semester. The Placement Cell is headed by Ms. Rhea Castellanos.
