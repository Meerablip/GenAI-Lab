Wellness Centre and health services

The Wellness Centre in Block F is open 24 hours a day. Consultations are free for students, and the centre has a 10-bed observation ward. Counselling is provided by three counsellors, and students can book up to 7 free sessions per semester through the Calmly app. Annual medical insurance with cover of 5,000 USD is included in the tuition fees. In an emergency, dial extension 100 from any campus phone to call the ambulance.
