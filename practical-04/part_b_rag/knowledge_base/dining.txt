Dining and mess facilities

The central dining hall, Tamarind Court, serves breakfast from 07:15 to 09:00, lunch from 12:30 to 14:15, and dinner from 19:30 to 21:15. On Sundays, a single brunch is served from 10:00 to 12:00 in place of breakfast and lunch. The monthly mess fee is 95 USD. Tamarind Court has vegetarian, vegan and Jain counters. During examination weeks, the late-night cafe Moth & Lantern stays open until 02:00. Outside food delivery is not allowed beyond the main gate after 21:30.
