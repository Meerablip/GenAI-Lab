Academic calendar 2025-26

For the 2025-26 academic year, the autumn semester starts on 4 August 2025 and ends on 12 December 2025. Mid-semester examinations are held from 22 to 26 September, and end-semester examinations begin on 1 December. Winter break runs from 15 December to 4 January. The spring semester starts on 12 January 2026 and ends on 8 May 2026. The convocation ceremony will be held on 20 June 2026 on the Clock Tower lawn.
