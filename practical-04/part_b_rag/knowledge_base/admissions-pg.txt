Postgraduate admissions

Postgraduate applicants to ZIT need a bachelor's degree with a minimum CGPA of 6.5 out of 10, or an equivalent percentage. The GRE is not required. Applications are accepted all year round for two intakes, one in January and one in August. Each applicant submits a statement of purpose of at most 600 words and is interviewed by a panel of three faculty members. The annual intake is 420 seats for the M.Tech programmes and 60 seats for the MBA programme. Applicants who hold a valid national research fellowship may apply directly to the doctoral programme without the interview round.
