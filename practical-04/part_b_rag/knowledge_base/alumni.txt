Alumni association

The ZIT alumni association, Windbreakers, was established in 1995 and has 41,000 members. It runs a mentorship programme that pairs final-year students with alumni. The Alumni Fund raised 2.3 million USD in 2025. Alumni Day is celebrated on the first Saturday of October. The 2024 Distinguished Alumnus Award went to Cornelia Abbasi, the founder of Brightwater Systems.
