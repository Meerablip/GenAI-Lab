Tuition and fees

Annual tuition for undergraduate engineering programmes at ZIT is 8,200 USD, and for design programmes it is 7,400 USD. Tuition is paid in two instalments, due on 15 July and 15 December. A late payment fee of 2% of the instalment is charged for every week of delay, up to a maximum of 10%. Hostel and mess charges are billed separately from tuition. Students who withdraw within 14 days of the start of the semester receive an 80% refund of the instalment; after that, no refund is given. A one-time caution deposit of 500 USD is refundable at graduation.
