International exchange programmes

ZIT has exchange agreements with 21 partner universities, including Nordvik University in Norway and the Koyo Institute in Japan. To be eligible, a student needs a CGPA of at least 8.2 and no active backlogs. Applications for the spring semester abroad are submitted in September. Selected students receive a travel grant of up to 1,800 USD. Credits earned at the partner university are transferred after approval by the Dean of Academics.
